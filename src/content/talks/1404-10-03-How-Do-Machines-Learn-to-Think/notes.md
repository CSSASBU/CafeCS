
>[!info] Presenter: Mohammad Reza Khan Mohammadi

> **Overview:** Modern AI has achieved strong results in pattern recognition, yet reasoning remains a fundamental challenge. This talk explores the mathematical connection between statistical learning and formal logic, focusing on neuro-symbolic AI, where deep learning is combined with structured reasoning. By uniting neural flexibility with logical precision, we aim to address key limitations of current models, including black-box behavior and weak logical consistency, enabling AI systems that are more reliable, interpretable, and capable of multi-step reasoning.
